The test now verifies the amendment feature spec artifact has a codex-based backend in its frontmatter, proving the planner executed with the re-resolved backend.

Since gemini is disabled in the mock setup, `resolve_completion_panel` logs a skip warning. Asserting exactly 1 occurrence proves panel resolution happens only at planning-time, not again at same-run completion entry.
