2. **Persisted quick-dev completion/status honored on reconstruction** (Required #2)

   - Updated `persist_quick_dev_state` to accept and persist both counter values alongside phase/status.
   - Updated `run_phase_machine` to initialize `review_iteration` and `final_review_attempts` from `state.quick_dev_review_iteration` and `state.quick_dev_final_review_attempts` instead of hardcoded `0`.
